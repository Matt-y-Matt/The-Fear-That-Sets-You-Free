The FearThat SetsYou Free

Scripture for when you’re afraid, stuck, or growing cold.

“Do not fear, for God has come to test you, that the fear of him may be before you, that you may not sin.”Exodus 20:20

Start here

The Bible talks about two kinds of fear. One makes you run from God, like Adam hiding in the garden. The other draws you close to him, like Moses walking into the thick darkness where God was (Exodus 20:21).

The first fear chains you. The second sets you free. It is the awe of knowing how holy and how good God is, and it makes every other fear smaller.

How the links work

“Read the whole chapter”

Opens the full chapter on Bible.com in the ESV. If you have the Bible App (YouVersion) on your phone, it may open there instead. Reading the whole chapter shows you who is speaking, to whom, and why, because a verse means what it means in its chapter.

“What does this mean?”

Opens a free commentary on that chapter by Pastor David Guzik (Enduring Word). It covers the whole chapter, so scroll down to find your verse number. Guzik quotes the New King James Version, so the wording will look slightly different.

What is a commentary?

A commentary is written by a Bible teacher who walks through Scripture verse by verse. It explains the background, the meaning of key words, and how a passage fits with the rest of the Bible. It’s like reading with an experienced teacher beside you.

Why use one?

The Bible was first written to people in a different time, language and culture. A good commentary fills in what they knew and we don’t, and it helps keep us from reading our own ideas into the text.

When should I read it?

Read the verse first, then the whole chapter, and ask what God is saying to you. Open the commentary when something is unclear, when you want to go deeper, or before you share the verse with someone else. Scripture first, commentary second.
